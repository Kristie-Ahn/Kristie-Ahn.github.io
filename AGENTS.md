# AGENTS.md

## Mission
Build and maintain a professional, accurate personal site that reflects the user’s CV and public professional profile without adding unverified details.

## Never publish
- Phone number, home address, or personal email address.
- Grades, salary, visa status, or date of birth.
- Anything in `Private/`. Make sure it is listed in `.gitignore` before any commit.
- Any other private or sensitive information the user has not explicitly provided, including family details, banking information, medical details, legal issues, or proprietary confidential information.
- Anything that would require guessing, such as made-up accomplishments, years, job titles, projects, or personal details not visible in the CV.
- Any content that is discriminatory, abusive, sexual, political, or otherwise inappropriate.
- Anything that impersonates, misrepresents, or invents the user’s professional identity.

## Ask me first
- Before any git push or any change on GitHub (repos, settings, Pages, releases).
- Before deleting or overwriting a file.
- Before adding or changing personal details beyond what is in the CV or explicitly provided by the user.
- Before publishing links, handles, or contact information not already confirmed by the user.
- Before rewriting CV content in a way that changes job titles, dates, duties, or section order.
- Before introducing branding, imagery, or visual elements that are not clearly consistent with the user’s preferences.

## Style
- Plain HTML and CSS, clean and minimal. No frameworks or build step.
- Portfolio as a gallery page that links to each project. Only list projects the user has confirmed.
- Columbia blue palette with restrained contrast and modern typography.
- Tone: professional, polished, and slightly casual.
- Streamlined navigation and a clean section structure; cards and tiles for easy scanning.
- Preserve the CV’s section order.
- Prefer clarity, brevity, and accuracy over decorative complexity.
- Pages must be readable on desktop and mobile.

## When you finish
- Tell me what you changed and what you skipped.
- Open the page in the browser.
- Do not push to GitHub without explicit approval.
